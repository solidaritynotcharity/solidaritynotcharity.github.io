On a journey to build better organisations, solidarity and movements

I'm a labour rights advocate and campaigner working across borders. I currently lead the Urgent Appeal System at the Clean Clothes Campaign, shaping global strategies in solidarity with workers.

My work spans labour rights, human rights defender protection, democratic movements, and corporate accountability. I have organised campaigns across East Asia, contributed to international human rights advocacy, facilitated symposia with civil society leaders, and written about the politics shaping Hong Kong and the region.

I also curate exhibitions and public programmes that make movements, resistance, and collective power tangible to wider audiences.
